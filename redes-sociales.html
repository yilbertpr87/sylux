Frequently Asked Questions

The questions every business owner asks us first.

Do I need to know how to edit videos or use apps?

No. You only send photos and videos from your phone, exactly as you took them, and approve what the agent prepares with a YES or a NO. We handle everything else.

Can something be published without my approval?

Never. Every post and every reel waits for your YES. If you say NO, it is fixed or discarded — your accounts, your rules.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. The $99 setup is free if you choose to start with 3 months — but that's your choice, not an obligation.

Who answers my private messages (DMs)?

You do — private conversations with your clients are personal, and we don't touch them. The agent answers public comments on Growth and Pro plans, in your tone.
