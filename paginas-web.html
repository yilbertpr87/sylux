Frequently Asked Questions

What every business owner asks us about their website.

Is the website really mine?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and the website is your property — if one day you leave SYLUX, your website goes with you. It is not a rented page inside someone else's platform.

I already book through Booksy / Fresha / Square. Can I keep it?

Yes. Your website's Book button can open your current booking system while you decide — and when you want everything under your own roof, we set up booking inside your own page. Your website wins either way: it is what Google shows.

Can I change photos and prices later?

Yes. With the optional $25/month maintenance, send us your new photos or prices and we update them — plus hosting and 1 small change per month are included. Without maintenance, changes are quoted as small adjustments.

How long until my website is live?

Once we have your photos, services and prices, a Business website is normally ready to review within days — you approve the preview on your phone before it goes live, always.
