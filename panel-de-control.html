Paying a Worker: Sign First, Then Download

This is how a payment works in your panel — built so you always keep proof.

1

You record the payment

Worker, amount, date and what the payment is for. It is saved in his profile and in your expenses at the same time.

2

The worker signs on screen

The payment document opens and the worker signs it with his finger on the phone or tablet. No signature, no download — the signature comes first, always.

3

You download the signed receipt

The signed receipt downloads as a clean PDF — one per worker, one per payment — and a copy stays in your panel forever.

Frequently Asked Questions

What every business owner asks us about the panel.

Do I need a website to have the panel?

No. The panel works on its own, with its own private login address. If you also have a SYLUX website, we add a 'Private Access' button that takes you straight into your panel — and with the bundle you save $149.

Can I add more workers later?

Yes. Add workers whenever your team grows — each with his photo, work plan, check-in/out record and payment history. You control each one independently.

Does the panel file my taxes?

No — and anyone who promises that is not being straight with you. The panel organizes your numbers all year and downloads your reports sorted by category (including per-worker payment summaries for independent contractors), so your accountant files fast and you stop paying for chaos.

What if I want a special button or function?

Tell us. Most day-to-day buttons and categories are part of your setup. Very advanced customization or brand-new functions are quoted in writing before we build them — you always know the price first.

Is my information safe?

Your panel uses encrypted passwords, a secure HTTPS connection, automatic lockout against password-guessing robots, optional two-step verification and daily backups. Each business is isolated: nobody can see another business's data, and we never store cards or bank accounts.
